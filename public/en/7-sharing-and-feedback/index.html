7. Sharing and Feedback

SHARING AND FEEDBACK

This section records feedback from my mentors at AWS and my personal suggestions to help improve the Bootcamp program for future participants.

1. Mentor’s Feedback

Throughout the process of completing the Lab series and weekly reports, I have received valuable assessments and guidance from my technical mentors:

  • Attitude: Acknowledged my proactive spirit, timely completion of Labs, and strong security awareness when configuring systems.
  • Technical Skills: Encouraged me to delve deeper into infrastructure automation solutions (such as Infrastructure as Code - IaC) and advanced cost optimization on AWS.

2. Personal Contributions & Suggestions

To help the Workforce Bootcamp - First Cloud AI Journey program become more comprehensive and provide greater value to future students, I would like to propose a few suggestions:

Regarding program content:

  • Adjust the curriculum balance or organize additional mini-workshops on practical, in-depth instruction for Generative AI (such as Amazon Bedrock) starting from the mid-weeks to give students more time to research and complete practical assignments.

Regarding instructional materials:

  • Include more simulated common error scenarios (troubleshooting scenarios) regarding S3 Bucket Policy and IAM JSON configurations so that future students can proactively handle these when encountering complex challenges.

3. End-of-Internship Survey

I have fully completed the program quality assessment survey provided by the AWS organizing committee to contribute objective data, helping to improve the Bootcamp’s operational processes.

🎯 Post-program Commitment: The knowledge and practical experience gained from this AWS Workshop will serve as a solid foundation for me to continue developing my expertise in Cloud Computing and Information Technology.
