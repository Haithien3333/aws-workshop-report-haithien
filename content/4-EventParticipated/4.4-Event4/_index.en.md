* **Objective:** To provide an ultimate hands-on environment for engineers, developers, and junior solution architects to experience the design, development, and optimization of next-generation Artificial Intelligence (Agentic AI) systems on cloud computing.

## I. HACKATHON SPIRIT & EXPERIENCE OVERVIEW

The event offered a practical and profound perspective on the 24-hour journey of continuous building, operating, and troubleshooting under high pressure. The core philosophy gathered throughout the program is:

The journey is not merely about celebrating the final outcome or winning awards; the greatest value lies in the real-world stumbles during debugging, system optimization, and the lessons learned in pushing personal limits to master new technologies.

## II. KEY LESSONS & PERSONAL TAKEAWAYS

Through the event's activities, important professional values were synthesized:

1. **Cloud Architecture Competency:** Mastered the mindset of designing native applications on AWS, learning how to balance processing performance, network latency, and operational costs for heavy AI workloads.
2. **Deep Understanding of Agentic AI:** Clarified the operation and collaboration mechanisms among autonomous AI agents to solve multi-step business problems, surpassing the limits of standalone Large Language Models (LLMs).
3. **Problem-Solving Skills:** Cultivated sharpness and composure under the time pressure of a hackathon; learned how to break down large problems into smaller modules for rapid handling and testing.
4. **Teamwork & Communication Skills:** Collaborated effectively with individuals possessing diverse technical strengths (ranging from programmers and data engineers to system architects) to achieve the project's common goals.
